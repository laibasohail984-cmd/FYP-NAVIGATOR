    Questions

    What is an FYP?

    A Final Year Project is the capstone work students complete in their last year of a degree, usually with a supervisor and a final defense.

    Is FYP Navigator free?

    Yes. Browse ideas, save favourites and follow the roadmap at no cost.

    Can I use an idea exactly as listed?

    Treat each idea as a starting point. Adapt the scope and add your own twist with your supervisor so the work is truly yours.

    How do I choose a good idea?

    Pick something you find interesting, check that the data or hardware is available, and make sure it fits your team size and timeline.
